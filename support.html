BookWorth Support

Help for the free public beta

Before Contacting Support

Confirm that you have an internet connection, reopen the app, and try the scan or search again. For clear photo results, use even lighting and keep the entire cover or copyright page in frame.

Accounts and Backup

Guest mode does not require an account. Signed-in book records sync when the Account screen says Cloud backup is current. Use Forgot password on that screen if you cannot sign in.

Delete an Account

Open Account & Backup, sign in, then choose Delete Account & Cloud Data. This permanently removes the account and its cloud collection.

Contact Support

Include your device model, iOS version, the action you were taking, and the exact error message. Do not include your password, Supabase key, or other credentials.

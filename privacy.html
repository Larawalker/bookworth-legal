Privacy Policy

Effective August 18, 2026

Your Choice

BookWorth works without an account. Guest collections stay on your device. If you create an optional account, BookWorth uses your email address to authenticate you and privately sync your saved book records.

Photos and Text Recognition

Photos submitted for cover or copyright-page recognition are sent securely to the BookWorth processing service. They are not publicly available, are deleted immediately after analysis, and abandoned uploads are deleted within 15 minutes. Extracted text is saved only when you choose to save the related book record.

Cloud Accounts

Supabase provides account authentication and cloud storage. BookWorth does not store your password. Signed-in collections, including saved edition analysis and valuation records, are associated with your account. Custom cover photos remain only on the device where you selected them.

Book and Market Services

Search terms, ISBNs, and book details may be sent to BookWorth's service providers, including Google Books, Open Library, and eBay, to identify books and find relevant market information. Hosting providers may process ordinary request information such as IP address and timestamps for security and reliability.

No Tracking or Sale

BookWorth does not sell personal information, run advertising, or track you across other companies' apps and websites. The beta does not include third-party advertising analytics.

Retention and Deletion

Guest records remain until you remove them or delete the app. Cloud records remain until you delete individual books or use Delete Account & Cloud Data in the Account screen. Account deletion permanently removes the account and its cloud library.

Children and Policy Changes

BookWorth is not directed to children under 13. This policy may be updated as the beta changes; the effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.

Contact

For privacy requests or questions, contact BookWorth through the support request form.
